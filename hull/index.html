Why BMB in Hull

Old Cottage.
Great Lot.
New Home in 12 Weeks.

If you're looking for a modular home builder in Hull, MA, odds are you're not staring at an empty lot — you're staring at a tired cottage on a great one. Hull is a narrow peninsula where the ocean is never more than a few blocks away, lots are small and tightly packed from Pemberton to Nantasket, and a huge share of the housing stock began life as seasonal beach cottages. The dominant build in Hull isn't new construction on raw land. It's the teardown-rebuild: take the lot you already love, remove the cottage that's past saving, and set a modern, elevated, year-round home in its place.

That build comes with rules. Many coastal lots in Hull sit in FEMA flood zones — including velocity zones, where new homes must be elevated on foundations engineered for wave action. This is where out-of-area contractors stall and budgets blow up. Mike Biviano is a 4th-generation contractor based just down the coast in Marshfield, with four decades of work across South Shore building departments and coastal permitting. He designs your home around the elevation requirements from day one, handles elevation certificates and NFIP compliance, and locks the whole thing — foundation included — into one fixed price per square foot before groundbreaking.

Modular has a second advantage that matters enormously on a dense peninsula: speed and footprint. Instead of a year of crews and dumpsters crammed onto a 5,000-square-foot lot, your home is built in the factory while the site and foundation are prepared, then craned into place in a single day. From groundbreaking to move-in runs 8–12 weeks, at $250 per square foot versus $400–$600 stick-built — typically $200,000–$300,000 in savings. See why modular construction beats stick-built on the coast and walk through our five-step build process, then call Mike about your Hull lot.
