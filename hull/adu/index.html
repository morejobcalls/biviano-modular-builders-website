ADUs in Hull

A Second Home
on Your Hull Lot.

On the Hull peninsula, nobody's lot is getting any bigger. But Massachusetts now allows ADUs up to 900 sq ft by right in single-family zoning statewide, and that changed the math for Hull homeowners: a compact, factory-built rental cottage at $250 per square foot, steps from Nantasket Beach, on land you already own. Summer rates are strong, and year-round tenants compete for the same scarce housing stock.

Building in Hull — Allerton, Kenberma, Hull Village, out toward Pemberton Point — means working with tight setbacks, sea-facing weather exposure, and FEMA flood zones on many coastal lots. Modular handles that well: compact 400–600 sq ft footprints fit lots that a sprawling stick-built addition never could, and the unit arrives weather-tight. Mike confirms siting, setbacks, and any elevation requirements during your free site walk — before plans are drawn, before contracts are signed, before you're financially committed.

Pricing is identical to our full modular homes: $250 per square foot, locked in writing before groundbreaking. A 600 sq ft ADU is approximately $150,000. A 900 sq ft ADU is approximately $225,000. Turnkey — design, permits, factory build, foundation, crane set, finish work, final inspection — through the same five-step process as a full build. Planning a full teardown-rebuild instead? What ADUs Are Used For

Six Ways
Hull Families
Use ADUs.

Same shell, very different lives inside. The best ADU is the one designed around what your family actually needs — not a generic template.

01
In-Law Suite

Bring aging parents home without giving up privacy on either side. Single-floor layouts with wider doorways, walk-in showers, and zero stairs — designed for aging in place from day one.

02
Rental Income

This is the Hull play. Nantasket-area summer rentals command real premiums, and year-round tenants compete for scarce housing on the peninsula. A code-compliant ADU turns your lot into an income property without selling a thing.

03
Home Office / Studio

A real, separated workspace — not a corner of the dining room. Insulated, climate-controlled, hard-wired internet. Walk across the yard, close the door, do the work.

04
Guest House

Everyone visits when you live by the beach. A proper guest house means summer company stays comfortable — and your main home stays yours. Off-season, it flexes back into an office, studio, or rental.

05
Adult Children Returning

For families whose adult kids are saving for a first home or finishing graduate school. Privacy for both households without the cost of separate housing during a transitional season.

06
Property Value Add

A permitted, code-compliant ADU adds documented square footage and a separate income stream on a peninsula where buildable land is effectively gone — and both of those appraise.
