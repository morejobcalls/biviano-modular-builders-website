Why BMB in Hanson

The Modular Home Builder
for Hanson's
Bigger Lots.

Hanson is the quiet value play on the South Shore. It's rural-suburban, inland, and honest — Wampatuck Pond, wooded acreage, a commuter rail station with direct service toward Boston, and land prices that make coastal-town buyers do a double take. Families who got priced out of Scituate, Duxbury, or Norwell aren't settling when they build in Hanson. They're trading a cramped lot near the water for a bigger home on more land, twenty minutes inland.

Run the numbers and the case gets stronger. Stick-built construction locally costs $400–$600 per square foot and takes 12–15 months. BMB builds the same custom home at $250 per square foot in 8–12 weeks — typical savings of $200,000 to $300,000. Stack that on top of Hanson's land prices and the same total budget that buys a dated three-bedroom on the coast buys a brand-new four-bedroom here, custom-designed, on an acre. That's the whole argument for building modular in one sentence.

Hanson's bigger lots come with rural realities: most are wooded, and most run on Title 5 septic rather than town sewer. That's normal here, and Mike handles all of it. With 40+ years building across the South Shore and working with its building departments, he quotes the full site package — clearing, grading, foundation, septic — locks it in writing, and manages the entire five-step build process from permits to crane set to keys.
