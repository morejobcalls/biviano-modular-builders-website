# Google Ads Campaign Blueprint — Biviano Modular Builders
**Client:** Biviano Modular Builders (modular brand of Biviano General Contracting LLC, Marshfield MA)
**Objective:** Lead generation — booked **Free Modular Home Design & Budget Consultations**
**Budget:** $1,000–2,500/mo (plan to ~$2,000/mo working figure)
**Geo:** Core South Shore MA towns
**Built:** 2026-06-03 · Pairs with `keyword-map-modular-southshore.md` (SEO) — this doc is the PAID/PPC plan.

---

## 1. Strategy & realistic expectations (read first)

Modular/home-build search is **low-volume, high-ticket, expensive-per-click**. Benchmarks: homebuilding avg CPC ~$5.85, Home & Improvement ~$7.85, construction CPL ~$165 (sources at bottom). High-intent modular terms ("modular home builder near me") run higher — assume **$8–15 CPC**.

**At ~$2,000/mo that's ~5–8 clicks/day → ~150–240 clicks/mo → ~6–15 leads/mo** (3–6% LP conversion), CPL ~$150–300. One closed modular home is six figures, so even a handful of qualified consults/month pays for itself many times over.

**Implication for a lean budget — this is the whole strategy:**
1. **Buy only bottom-funnel, commercial-intent clicks.** No informational/research terms on paid (those go to SEO/organic — see the keyword map). Every dollar goes to "ready to hire/quote" searchers.
2. **Negative keywords are priority #1**, not an afterthought — "modular" leaks budget to furniture, commercial buildings, mobile/manufactured homes, jobs, DIY. The negative list below is the single biggest spend-protector.
3. **Tight match types** — exact + phrase only. No broad match at this budget.
4. **One purpose-built landing page** with a self-qualifying offer (filters tire-kickers — high-ticket means lead *quality* > quantity).
5. **Track form submits + calls + booked consults** (offline import) so we optimize to real pipeline, not vanity clicks.

```
Campaign: Search — Modular — South Shore (Search Network only)
├── Ad Group 1: Modular Home Builder (geo/commercial)      ◀ LAUNCH (Phase 1)
├── Ad Group 2: Custom Modular Homes (commercial)           ◀ LAUNCH (Phase 1)
├── Ad Group 3: Modular ADU                                 ◀ Phase 2 (add when budget/data allow)
└── Ad Group 4: Modular Home Cost / Pricing                 ◀ Phase 2 (watch — leans research)

```
**Lean launch = Ad Groups 1 & 2 only.** Add 3–4 once you have ~20–30 conversions of data and/or more budget. Keep it ONE campaign so the limited budget isn't split thin.

## 8. Conversion tracking — BUILD FROM SCRATCH (not currently set up)
Track three things; optimize to the deepest one you can.

**A. Form submit (primary, online):**
- Put the LP form behind a **gtag/GTM** event. On successful submit, fire a Google Ads conversion ("Modular — LP Lead"). Easiest reliable method: Google Tag via GTM, trigger on the form's success state / thank-you view.
- Our site form posts to GHL via webhook (no-cors) — for the LP, add a dedicated conversion event on submit (don't rely on a redirect we don't control).

**B. Phone calls (primary, online):**
- **Calls from ads:** enable call reporting on the Call asset → counts as conversion.
- **Calls from the LP:** turn on **Google forwarding number** (call-from-website conversion) so LP calls ≥ a min duration count.

**C. Booked/qualified consult (the money metric — offline import):**
- Capture **GCLID** on the LP (auto-tagging on + hidden `gclid` field in the form) → pass to GHL as a contact field.
- When a lead becomes a **booked consultation** (GHL pipeline stage), **upload an offline conversion** to Google Ads ("Modular — Booked Consult") via GHL→Google Ads conversion export or a scheduled offline-conversion upload keyed on GCLID.
- **Set "Booked Consult" as the primary conversion** once volume allows → Smart Bidding optimizes to real pipeline, not raw form fills. Form/call = secondary.

**Setup checklist:** auto-tagging ON · Google Ads ↔ GA4 linked · GTM on the LP · conversions created (LP Lead, Call, Booked Consult) · gclid capture + GHL field · offline import wired.

## 9. Landing page + offer
**Destination:** a **dedicated, purpose-built LP** on the modular site (e.g., `bivianomodularbuilders.com/free-consultation/`). Do NOT send paid traffic to the generic homepage — message-match to the ad lifts conversion + Quality Score.

**Offer (merged from your picks):** **"Free Modular Home Design & Budget Consultation."** Frames as design+budget (attracts real planners) while staying an easy yes.

**LP structure (above the fold → down):**
1. **Hero:** "Free Modular Home Design & Budget Consultation" + subhead: "South Shore's family modular builder for 40+ years. Custom homes, 20–30% less than stick-built, in as little as 8–12 weeks." + form (right/below) + click-to-call.
2. **Self-qualifying form** (the quality filter): Full Name, Phone, Email, Town, **Do you own the land?** (Yes / Under contract / Still looking), **Timeline** (0–6 / 6–12 / 12+ mo), **Project type** (New home / ADU / Not sure), optional budget range. + hidden `gclid`.
3. **Why modular w/ Biviano:** 20–30% savings, 8–12 weeks, no weather delays, one team permit→finish.
4. **Proof:** 5.0★ · 30 Google reviews, finished-home photos, 40+ yrs, licensed & insured, FEMA/coastal, Trex.
5. **How it works** (3–4 steps) + second CTA.
6. **FAQ** (cost, customization, timeline, financing) + final CTA + phone.
- Fast, mobile-first, click-to-call sticky on mobile, match headline to the ad's offer.

## 10. Budget & bid ramp
- **Daily:** ~$66/day ($2k/mo). Floor $33/day ($1k) — at the floor, run Ad Group 1 only.
- **Split:** ~60% Ad Group 1 (builder/geo) / 40% Ad Group 2 (custom) — concentrate on the highest-intent first. (Single campaign → control via ad-group bids/keyword priority.)
- **Bid ramp:** Maximize Clicks (capped ~$12–15) or Manual CPC → gather ~15–30 conversions → **Maximize Conversions**, then **tCPA** once CPL stabilizes.
- Expect a 2–4 week learning/negative-mining period before CPL settles.

---

## 11. Launch checklist & optimization cadence
**Pre-launch:** [ ] LP live + mobile-tested [ ] conversion tracking firing (test lead) [ ] gclid capture verified [ ] call reporting on [ ] negatives shared-list applied [ ] geo = presence-only [ ] Display/Partners OFF [ ] assets/extensions added [ ] billing set.

**Weeks 1–2:** mine Search Terms report 2–3×/wk → add negatives aggressively; pause zero-converting high-spend terms; confirm conversions recording.
**Weeks 3–4:** assess CPL by ad group/keyword; shift budget to winners; add 2nd RSA variant; bid adjustments (device/geo/schedule).
**Day 30/60/90:** 30 — stable tracking + clean search terms + first CPL read. 60 — switch to Maximize Conversions; add Ad Group 3 (ADU) if budget allows. 90 — tCPA targeting Booked-Consult; consider Tier-2 towns.

**KPIs:** Cost/Lead (target <$200), **Cost/Booked Consult** (the real one), Lead→Consult rate, Consult→Contract rate, ROAS on closed homes. At lean budget, **judge on consults booked, not clicks.**
