**Client:** Biviano Modular Builders (modular brand of Biviano General Contracting LLC, Marshfield MA)
**Deliverable:** Phase 1 — SEO, authority & content framework
**Goal:** Rank organically for "modular home builder [town]" style searches across the South Shore service area.
**Last updated:** 2026-06-03

---

## 1. Overview — how to use this

This is a **map**, not just a list. Keywords are grouped by **search intent** (what the searcher wants), then mapped to the **page** that should target them. Use it to:
- Decide which pages to build/optimize first (see the Priority Roadmap, §5).
- Write each page to a clear primary keyword + a cluster of supporting terms.
- Spin up town landing pages and "modular vs stick-built in [Town]" articles systematically.

**A note on volumes:** modular-home local search volumes are low-to-moderate and not reliably published per-town. Rather than fabricate numbers, each cluster is rated by **intent value** (how close to hiring) and **winnability** (how easy for a local site to rank). Lead with high-intent + high-winnability first.

### Service-area town list (tiered)
- **Tier 1 — Core (build/optimize first):** Marshfield, Scituate, Duxbury, Pembroke, Norwell, Hanover, Hingham
- **Tier 2 — Near South Shore (next):** Cohasset, Hull, Kingston, Plymouth, Hanson, Halifax
- **Tier 3 — Expansion (later):** Plympton, Carver, Whitman, Rockland, Abington, Weymouth, Braintree
- **Marshfield hyperlocal villages (future/secondary):** Humarock, Brant Rock, Green Harbor, Ocean Bluff

Biviano's home turf is **Marshfield** — Mike is a lifelong resident known to local inspectors and boards. That local E-E-A-T is the single biggest ranking advantage; win Marshfield + immediate neighbors before reaching for Boston-adjacent towns.

## 5. Priority roadmap (first 12 moves, ~weeks 1–12)

1. **Optimize the homepage** for "modular home builder South Shore MA" + Marshfield (title, H1, meta, intro copy, internal links).
2. **Marshfield town page** — home turf, easiest first ranking win; load with local proof (builds, boards, inspectors).
3. **"Modular vs Stick-Built in Marshfield" article** — near-zero competition, kills the cost+stigma objection, feeds the town page via internal links. Highest content ROI.
4. **Cost / Pricing page + calculator** — owns "modular home cost MA / cost per square foot," answers "can we afford this?", captures leads.
5. **Scituate town page.**
6. **Duxbury town page.**
7. **"Modular vs Stick-Built" templated for Scituate & Duxbury** (reuse the Marshfield template).
8. **Modular Financing / appraisal page** — fills a real objection gap competitors under-serve.
9. **Modular ADU page** — rising MA ADU demand; use existing drone/build footage as proof.
10. **Pembroke, Norwell, Hanover, Hingham town pages** (Tier 1 fill).
11. **Informational blog cluster** — "what is a modular home," "are modular homes good quality," "do modular homes hold value" (Cluster C authority + internal links).
12. **Tier 2 towns + Marshfield hyperlocal villages** once Tier 1 starts ranking.

**Sequencing logic:** commercial intent + home-turf winnability first (1–6), then the comparison/cost content that converts the undecided (3,4,7), then objection pages (8,9), then breadth (10–12). Every new town/comparison page should internally link up to the homepage and across to the cost + comparison pillars.
