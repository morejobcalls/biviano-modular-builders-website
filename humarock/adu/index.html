What ADUs Are Used For

Four Ways
Humarock Families
Use ADUs.

Same shell, very different lives inside. The best ADU is the one designed around what your family actually needs — not a generic template.

01
In-Law Suite

Bring aging parents home without giving up privacy on either side. Single-floor layouts with wider doorways, walk-in showers, and zero stairs — designed for aging in place from day one.

02
Rental Income

A 600–900 sq ft ADU in Humarock rents for $1,800–$2,800/month long-term. Coastal towns command significantly more on short-term platforms during summer. Payback in 6–9 years is typical.

03
Home Office / Studio

A real, separated workspace — not a corner of the dining room. Insulated, climate-controlled, hard-wired internet. Walk across the yard, close the door, do the work.

04
Guest House

Visiting family. College kids home for summer. Friends in town. A proper guest house means your main home stays your main home — no fold-out couches, no shared bathrooms.

05
Adult Children Returning

For families whose adult kids are saving for a first home or finishing graduate school. Privacy for both households without the cost of separate housing during a transitional season.

06
Property Value Add

A permitted, code-compliant ADU adds documented square footage and a separate income stream — both of which appraise. For Humarock properties, well-designed ADUs typically add $200K–$350K to appraised value.

Humarock ADU Questions

Questions About
Building an ADU in Humarock.

Humarock ADUs are technically permitted through Marshfield's town bylaws and follow Marshfield's process for permitting and septic. Wind zone compliance is mandatory (110 mph rating standard). Flood elevation may apply on lots within FEMA velocity zones — Mike confirms all of this during your free site walk.
Modular ADUs in Humarock are priced at the same $250 per square foot as our full custom modular homes. A 600 sq ft ADU is approximately $150,000. A 900 sq ft ADU is approximately $225,000. That includes Mike handling design, permits, factory build, foundation, crane set, and finish work — turnkey.
ADUs in Humarock typically take 8–12 weeks from contract to keys. The unit is precision-built in a climate-controlled factory while Mike handles permitting and site prep on your property in parallel — including any Humarock-specific zoning or conservation review.
Mike handles all permitting, zoning verification, septic capacity confirmation, and town approvals as part of every Humarock ADU build. With 40+ years on the South Shore and personal relationships with every local inspector, he knows what each Humarock department requires before we submit.
