Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026

Biviano General Contracting LLC, doing business as Biviano Modular Builders ("we", "us"), runs bivianomodularbuilders.com. This page explains what information we collect when you contact us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • What you type into a form: your name, phone number, email address, town, and details about your project.
  • What you tell us on a call, text or email.
  • Basic visit information collected by cookies and similar tools from Google Analytics and Google Ads, such as the pages you viewed and the ad you clicked to reach us.

How we use it

  • To reply to your request, schedule your consultation and talk with you about your project.
  • To send you appointment reminders and project information by phone, text or email.
  • To understand which of our ads and pages are useful, so we can improve them.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We share it only with the service providers that help us run the business, such as our customer relationship software, our phone and text messaging provider, and Google's advertising and analytics tools, and only so they can perform those services for us. Phone numbers and text messaging consent are never shared with third parties for their own marketing. We may also disclose information when the law requires it.

Text messages

If you give us your phone number, we may text you about your request and appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop texts, or HELP for help.

Cookies and ads

Google may use cookies to show our ads and to measure whether an ad led to an inquiry. You can manage ad personalization at adssettings.google.com and block cookies in your browser settings.

Keeping it safe

Our forms are sent over a secure (https) connection. We keep your information only as long as we need it to serve you and to meet our legal and business obligations.

Your choices

You can ask us to see, correct or delete the information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you, by calling or emailing us below.
