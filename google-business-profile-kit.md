## ⚠️ 0. READ FIRST — The duplicate-listing / brand-vs-parent decision

**The situation:** BMB shares its **exact address and phone** with the parent company, **Biviano General Contracting LLC**, which already has a verified, established Google Business Profile (the "5.0★ · 30 Google reviews" cited in the ads blueprint, plus 21 five-star Birdeye reviews). Google does **not** allow two profiles at the same address/phone for the **same real-world business** — and it actively merges or suspends perceived duplicates. A second profile with identical NAP is the single biggest suspension risk in this whole project.

**There are two legitimate paths. Pick ONE — recommendation below.**

### ▶ RECOMMENDED — Path A: Do NOT create a second GBP. Optimize the parent profile + use a category/sub-org strategy.

Google's own guidance: a business gets **one** profile per real-world location, *unless* it operates **distinct departments/brands** that have a **separate public-facing name, a separate dedicated phone line, and separate hours.** BMB does not (yet) have a separate phone line — it shares (617) 678-6446. So the clean, suspension-proof move is:

1. **Keep ONE profile** at 2183 Ocean Street.
2. **Rename it (or keep parent name) and lean the categories/services/description toward modular** — because modular is the new paid-growth focus. Recommended profile name options, in order:
   - **Best (if Mike will adopt it as the d/b/a):** `Biviano Modular Builders` — clean brand match to the domain and the ads. Requires the name to reflect real-world signage/branding (add a small "Biviano Modular Builders" sign/decal or vehicle lettering so it's defensible if Google asks).
   - **Safe fallback (no rebrand):** `Biviano General Contracting` with **General Contractor** primary + **Modular Home Builder** secondary categories, and a modular-forward description. The domain still points paid + organic modular traffic to bivianomodularbuilders.com.
   - **Do NOT** keyword-stuff the name (e.g., "Biviano Modular Builders | South Shore Custom Modular Homes"). That's a Google name-policy violation and a common cause of suspension/ranking penalty.
3. **Link the profile's website field to `https://bivianomodularbuilders.com`** (the modular brand site) — this is allowed and ties the profile's authority to the modular domain for the paid launch.
4. This profile becomes the **Location asset** in Google Ads (blueprint §7) and the review destination for the proof machine.

**Why A is recommended:** zero suspension risk, instantly inherits the existing 30-review / 5.0★ prominence (a brand-new profile starts at zero reviews and ranks nowhere for 3–6 months), and Modular Home Builder is available as a category on the existing profile. You get 95% of the brand benefit with none of the duplicate risk.

### ▶ Path B (only if Mike truly wants a separate modular entity): Create a genuinely distinct profile.

Permissible ONLY if BMB becomes a **distinguishable business**: a **separate dedicated phone number** (e.g., a new tracking/DID line published as BMB's), a registered **d/b/a** ("Biviano Modular Builders"), and ideally distinct signage. Then:
- New profile: name `Biviano Modular Builders`, new phone, same address (allowed when the brands are genuinely distinct), website bivianobmodularbuilders.com.
- **Cost:** starts at zero reviews/prominence; needs its own citation set; risks Google merging it back into the parent if it can't tell them apart. Expect a manual verification (video).
- **Only choose B if** the modular line will scale into its own crew/brand with its own phone. Otherwise A wins.
