# BMB Voice — Tone

How Mike Biviano / Biviano Modular Builders sounds in every post, page, reply, and email.

## Core register
- Direct, no-BS, human. A contractor talking across a kitchen table, not a brand "crafting messaging."
- Confident from experience, never salesy. He doesn't convince — he explains, and the math convinces.
- Warm toward families, blunt about the industry ("you've been quoted a million dollars and 14 months — that's the problem").
- Light dry humor, used sparingly. Never jokey, never memes, never exclamation-point enthusiasm.

## Hard rules
- Specific numbers ALWAYS: "8–12 weeks," "$250/sq ft," "$200,000–$300,000 saved" — never "faster," "cheaper," "significant savings."
- Short sentences. Plain words. If a homeowner wouldn't say it on a job site, cut it.
- Kill the trailer stigma with facts, not defensiveness: same MA code (780 CMR), permanent foundation, appraises like site-built.
- Proof over claims: 4th generation, since the 1960s, 21 five-star reviews, BuildZoom 101, former Planning Board chair.
- One CTA, stated plainly: free 60-minute consultation with a real cost breakdown. 8 slots a month.

## Never
- Agency-speak: "elevate," "dream big," "unlock," "seamless," "journey," "solutions."
- Hype or urgency theater: no countdowns, no "act now," no fake scarcity beyond the real 8 slots/month.
- Hedging: no "we strive to," "we aim to." He does it or he doesn't say it.
- ALL CAPS, emoji strings, hashtag piles (one or two local tags max on social).

## Cadence example (the sound to match)
"A whole house, craned into place in one day. The modules were built in the factory while we poured the foundation and pulled the permits. Same code as stick-built. About $250 a square foot instead of $400-plus. That's the whole trick — two things happening at once instead of one thing happening slowly."
