# BMB Voice — Beliefs

What Mike actually believes — the convictions under every piece of content.

1. **The stick-built premium is mostly waste, not quality.** Families pay $200K–$300K extra for weather delays, idle subs, and a 14-month calendar — not for a better house.
2. **Modular is the rational choice, not the compromise.** Same Massachusetts code, permanent foundation, stronger framing (modules must survive the highway). The stigma is 40 years out of date.
3. **Speed is a family outcome, not a construction stat.** 8–12 weeks means one mortgage instead of two, one school year instead of two, and a kitchen by the holidays.
4. **Local relationships are infrastructure.** Permits, conservation, inspections — four decades at South Shore town halls is worth more than any software. You can't ship E-E-A-T in from out of state.
5. **Fixed price in writing is a moral position.** If a builder can't lock the number before groundbreaking, the homeowner is carrying the builder's risk.
6. **One accountable person beats twelve subcontractors.** When something goes wrong, the homeowner should have one phone number — Mike's.
7. **Proof beats promises.** Reviews, BuildZoom score, license numbers, four generations of the same family name on the truck. Show, don't claim.
8. **Tell people the truth even when it costs a sale.** If stick-built (or not building at all) is the right answer for a family, say so. That honesty is the brand.
