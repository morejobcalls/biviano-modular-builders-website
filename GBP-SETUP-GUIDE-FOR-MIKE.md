# Google Business Profile Setup — Action Guide for Mike

**What this is:** the step-by-step to get Biviano Modular Builders onto Google Maps. Right now, when someone on the South Shore searches "modular home builder near me" or "ADU builders near me" (3,600 searches/month), BMB doesn't exist on the map. This fixes that. It's the single highest-impact 45 minutes you can spend on the modular brand.

**What you need:** a laptop, logged into the **same Google account that manages the Biviano General Contracting profile** · your phone (for photos) · ~45 minutes.

**Golden rule for every field:** everything you enter must be real and defensible. No stretching. Google suspends contractor profiles constantly, and recovery takes months.

---

## PART 1 — Create the profile (10 min)

**1.** Go to **business.google.com** → make sure the account switcher (top right) shows the account that owns the Biviano General Contracting profile → click **Add business → Create a business profile**.

**2. Business name — type exactly:**
```
Biviano Modular Builders
```
Nothing extra. Not "Biviano Modular Builders – South Shore's #1..." — Google bans keyword-stuffed names and it's the #1 cause of suspension.

**3. Business category — type and select:**
```
Modular home builder
```
(This is the single most important field on the whole profile. More categories get added in Part 3.)

**4. "Do you want to add a location customers can visit?"** → **Yes** → enter:
```
2183 Ocean Street, Marshfield, MA 02050
```

**5. Service areas** — when asked "where do you serve customers?", add these 8 towns:
```
Marshfield MA · Scituate MA · Duxbury MA · Pembroke MA · Norwell MA · Hanover MA · Hingham MA · Humarock MA
```

**6. Contact info:**
- Phone: **(617) 678-6446**
- Website: **https://bivianomodularbuilders.com**

**7. Verification.** Google will offer postcard (5–14 days to arrive) or, more likely for contractors, **video verification**. If video:
- One continuous take, no cuts, landscape, 30–60 seconds
- Show: the building/signage at 2183 Ocean Street → walk inside → show your truck with Biviano lettering, tools/materials → open your business software or an invoice on screen (proof you run the business — nothing with customer personal info)
- Calm and boring is perfect. Don't narrate a pitch.

⚠️ **Important while waiting for verification:** do NOT edit the address or name. Continue with Part 2–4 — everything else can be filled in.

⚠️ **One-time signage task:** since BMB shares the address and phone with Biviano General Contracting, Google may ask for proof BMB is real. Order a small "Biviano Modular Builders" decal for the truck or a sign for the office. Cheap insurance — do it this week.

## PART 5 — Hours, attributes, photos (10 min)

**Hours:** set your real working hours (e.g., Mon–Fri 7:00 AM–5:00 PM, Sat by appointment). Don't list 24/7 unless calls actually get answered 24/7.

**Attributes** (Edit profile → More): check everything that's true —
- Family-owned ✓ (4 generations — say yes)
- Locally owned & operated ✓
- Online estimates ✓
- Appointment required ✓
- Veteran-owned — ONLY if true
- Payment types / financing — whatever you actually accept

**Photos — minimum 20 today, keep adding 3–5 a week.** Profiles with 100+ photos get ~5x more calls. From your phone, upload:
1. Crane/set-day shots (these are gold — film EVERY set day from now on)
2. Finished home exteriors (the "this is not a trailer" proof)
3. Interiors — kitchens, living rooms, baths
4. You on a job site (people hire people)
5. The truck with Biviano lettering
6. Logo → the **Logo** slot · best finished exterior → **Cover** photo

Real photos only. No stock images, ever — Google detects them and they kill trust.
