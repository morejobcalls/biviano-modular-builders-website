Why BMB in Cohasset

No Empty Lots?
No Problem.
We Make Lots Work.

If you're searching for a modular home builder in Cohasset, MA, you've probably already discovered the town's defining problem: there's almost nothing left to build on. Cohasset is one of the smallest, most sought-after coastal towns on the South Shore — a granite-edged harbor village where ferry commuters reach Boston in under an hour and buildable land essentially doesn't come up for sale. What does come up: older homes on remarkable lots along Jerusalem Road, near Sandy Beach, in Beechwood, and around the village. The play in Cohasset is the teardown-rebuild — and that's a play BMB runs constantly.

Mike Biviano is a 4th-generation contractor based one town down the coast in Marshfield, and his family has been building on the South Shore since the 1960s. Over four decades he's worked across South Shore building departments, conservation commissions, and design review boards — the exact gauntlet a Cohasset build has to clear. Rocky ledge under the topsoil, wetland buffers, tight setbacks near the historic village: these are the things that scare off out-of-area contractors and inflate stick-built quotes past $1M. Mike prices them honestly, plans around them, and locks your number in writing. You can see exactly what a custom modular home costs per square foot before you commit to anything.

The modular math hits especially hard in Cohasset. At $250 per square foot versus $400–$600 for local stick-built, families routinely keep $200,000–$300,000 — money better spent on the lot itself in a town this expensive. And because the factory builds your home while the site work and any ledge removal happen simultaneously, you're in your new Cohasset home 8–12 weeks from groundbreaking instead of carrying two housing payments for 12–15 months. If you're still weighing the approach, read why modular construction beats stick-built and walk through our five-step build process — then bring Mike the lot everyone else called impossible.
